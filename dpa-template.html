DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM / TEMPLATE v0.1

Contract the actual data flow.

This is a public contracting template—not an executed agreement and not legal advice. It is designed to accelerate enterprise review and must be completed for the actual services, roles, jurisdictions, retention terms and transfer mechanism before signature.

Template v0.1 · 22 Sep 2026Legal review requiredGDPR Article 28 structureDPDP-aware contracting
Draft status. Publication does not create a data-processing agreement. The parties should complete schedules, verify applicable law and obtain appropriate legal review before execution.

1. Parties, scope and precedence

This Addendum is between the enterprise customer identified in the applicable order, statement of work or master agreement (“Customer”) and Syntropix Technologies (OPC) Private Limited (“Syntropix”). It applies only to personal data Syntropix processes on behalf of Customer as part of the identified services. Processing for a separate independent purpose is not automatically governed by processor terms.

2. Roles and documented instructions

For processing performed on Customer’s behalf, Customer acts as controller or equivalent responsible party and Syntropix acts as processor or equivalent service provider, except where the actual data flow or applicable law requires another classification. Syntropix will process such data only on documented instructions, including agreed service configuration and written support requests, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

3. Confidentiality

Syntropix will ensure that persons authorised to process Customer personal data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations and receive access only where required for their responsibilities.

4. Security measures

Syntropix will maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to relevant processing risks. The executed schedule should identify measures actually implemented for the contracted service, such as role-based access, server-side privileged secrets, database authorization, release controls, logging/auditability, backup/continuity controls and incident procedures.

5. Subprocessors

Customer authorises Syntropix to use subprocessors identified in the agreed schedule. Where required by law or contract, Syntropix will provide notice of material additions or replacements and a reasonable opportunity to raise documented data-protection objections. Appropriate data-protection obligations will be imposed on subprocessors for services they perform.

6. Individual-rights support

Taking into account the nature of processing, Syntropix will provide reasonable assistance enabling Customer to respond to applicable access, correction, deletion, grievance or other individual-rights requests where relevant personal data is processed by Syntropix on Customer’s behalf.

7. Security incidents

Syntropix will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting Customer personal data processed under this Addendum and provide reasonably available information needed for Customer’s legal assessment and notification obligations. The parties may agree a more specific contractual notification target where appropriate.

8. DPIA, consultation and compliance assistance

Taking into account the nature of processing and information available to Syntropix, Syntropix will provide reasonable assistance with data-protection impact assessments, regulator consultation and other processor-assistance obligations applicable to the contracted processing.

9. Return and deletion

At termination or expiry, subject to applicable law and documented contractual retention requirements, Syntropix will delete or return Customer personal data according to the agreed schedule. Backup or legal-hold copies may remain for a limited period where technically or legally necessary and remain protected from ordinary use.

10. Audit and information rights

Syntropix will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with agreed processor obligations. Audit scope, confidentiality, frequency, independent-assurance alternatives, remediation and cost allocation should be agreed proportionately.

11. International transfers

The parties will identify whether personal data is transferred across relevant jurisdictions and use the transfer mechanism required by applicable law. For EU/EEA restricted transfers, this may include the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses where appropriate. Publication of this template does not activate any SCC module.

12. India DPDP alignment

Where India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and notified rules apply, the parties will identify Data Fiduciary/Data Processor responsibilities for the actual flow and update operational and contractual terms as phased provisions become applicable. Nothing here represents a future-dated obligation as already in force.

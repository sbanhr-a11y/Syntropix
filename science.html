EVIDENCE GOVERNANCE / LIVING RESEARCH REGISTER

Evidence before adjectives.

Published theory can justify what we investigate. It cannot, by itself, establish reliability, validity, fairness or predictive utility for a proprietary Syntropix instrument.

CLAIMS LADDER

Stronger language requires stronger evidence.

The intended use, population, language, version and decision context determine what can responsibly be claimed.

LEVEL 1

Research-informed

The construct and design draw on published literature. No instrument-specific psychometric claim is implied.

LEVEL 2

Evidence-informed developmental use

Items, scoring and interpretation have a documented rationale and may be used for development while evaluation continues.

LEVEL 3

Psychometrically evaluated

Used only when version-specific analyses support stated measurement properties, with sample and limitation disclosure.

LEVEL 4

Validated for a consequential use

Reserved for a defined use, population and decision context when evidence supports that exact interpretation.

Current guardrail. Unless an instrument-specific technical note states otherwise, Syntropix scores are developmental indicators—not diagnoses, clinical thresholds, neurological measurements, population percentiles or guaranteed predictors of performance.

VALIDATION ROADMAP

What must happen before claims get stronger.

Evidence attaches to the exact instrument version evaluated. A redesign, scoring change or new population may require new evidence.

From construct rationale to defensible interpretation.

We separate design logic from validation evidence and explicitly track what remains unknown.

01

Content validity

Construct map, expert review and cognitive interviewing.

02

Response & reliability evidence

Missingness, item behaviour, internal consistency and uncertainty.

03

Structure & external evidence

Factor structure, convergent/discriminant relationships and test–retest where appropriate.

04

Fairness & comparability

Differential item functioning, cross-group and cross-language evaluation.

05

Criterion evidence when needed

Only when the intended use requires criterion or incremental validity.

WHAT WE WILL NOT PRETEND

Developmental reflection is not diagnosis.

That boundary is especially important for consumer-facing instruments touching relationships, self-regulation, attention or identity.

RELATIONSHIP & INTIMACY

Relationship & Intimacy Blueprint

Uses dimensional relationship research as a design reference. It does not assign a fixed attachment identity or diagnose a relationship problem.

SHADOW & SELF-REGULATION

Shadow Pattern Map

Uses “shadow” as a developmental metaphor. It does not label users narcissistic, psychopathic, Machiavellian, toxic or disordered.

ATTENTION & REWARD

Attention & Reward Rhythm Map

Describes everyday patterns in attention, task initiation and reward responsiveness. It is not an ADHD, autism or neurological screening tool.

DATA-TO-EVIDENCE RULE

Participant data does not automatically become a norm.

With appropriate notice and consent, versioned data can support validation. Until adequate evidence exists, scores remain within-scale developmental descriptions.

THE PUBLICATION STANDARD

Five questions before an outcome becomes marketing.

Case evidence should help a buyer understand what happened—not merely persuade them that something worked.

01 / CONTEXT

Who and what problem?

Population, business context, baseline condition and relevant constraints.

02 / INTERVENTION

What actually changed?

The assessment, journey, coaching, simulation, manager action or system introduced.

03 / MEASUREMENT

How was movement observed?

Timing, instruments, operational metrics, comparison basis and missing-data considerations.

04 / ATTRIBUTION

What can we reasonably infer?

A before/after change does not prove Syntropix caused the outcome. The evaluation design determines the strength of the claim.

05 / LIMITATIONS

What should the reader not conclude?

Sample limitations, confounds, self-selection, short follow-up windows and contextual boundaries remain visible.

CURRENT PROOF LAYERS

Different evidence supports different conclusions.

Syntropix separates practitioner perspective, product evidence, deployment evidence and business-outcome evidence instead of blending them into one credibility claim.

A

Practitioner perspectives

Named testimonials describe the perceived value of the approach. They are not effectiveness studies.

PUBLISHED
B

Instrument evidence

Construct rationale, item behaviour, reliability, structure, fairness and external relationships are evaluated by instrument version.

BUILDING
C

Deployment evidence

Participation, completion, application, behaviour follow-through and operational signals can be measured within a client engagement.

CLIENT-SPECIFIC
D

Outcome evidence

Claims about retention, performance, sales or other business outcomes require an evaluation design appropriate to that inference.

EARNED, NOT ASSUMED

ILLUSTRATIVE ENGAGEMENT ARCHITECTURES

Show the system before claiming the result.

The examples below illustrate how Syntropix can structure an engagement. They are not presented as customer case studies or outcome claims.

01 / MANAGERS

Manager capability journey

Baseline developmental assessment → cohort pattern review → focused learning/practice → manager experiments → coaching checkpoints → follow-up behaviour evidence. Possible measures include completion, behavioural self-report, stakeholder feedback and agreed operating indicators.

02 / TALENT

Horizon AC/DC

Role/competency architecture → simulations → assessor evidence → calibration → development priorities → participant feedback → governance review. Evidence focuses on observable behaviour and assessor process quality, not a single automated verdict.

03 / EXPERIENCE

The Culture Compass

Question design → privacy review → pulse deployment → pattern analysis → action taxonomy → owner/due-date tracking → follow-up pulse. Survey movement is interpreted alongside organizational context rather than represented as proof of causality.

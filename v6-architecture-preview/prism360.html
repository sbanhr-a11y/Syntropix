PRISM360

Feedback made actionable.

A multi-rater development experience that surfaces patterns across relevant workplace perspectives and translates selected priorities into a focused development plan.

Discuss Prism360 →

EXPERIENCE ARCHITECTURE

Perspective → pattern → action.

01 / INPUT

Relevant perspectives

Self, manager, peers, direct reports and other stakeholders can be configured around the programme purpose.

02 / SAFEGUARDS

Responsible reporting

Small rater groups are suppressed where aggregation would not provide an appropriate confidentiality safeguard.

03 / INTERPRETATION

Patterns, not verdicts

Feedback identifies development themes; it is not a clinical diagnostic or automatic employment decision.

04 / ACTION

30–60–90 focus

Selected priorities become behavior experiments, coaching conversations and a review cadence.

A minimum group threshold is a reporting safeguard, not an absolute guarantee of anonymity. Design must also consider role context, subgroup size and potentially identifying comments.

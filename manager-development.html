COACHING GOVERNANCE

A network with standards.

Empanelment

Coach selection should document experience, references, coaching education/credentials where relevant, domain fit, confidentiality commitments, conflicts of interest and client-specific requirements. Syntropix will not imply that a coach credential transfers to Syntropix.

Confidentiality boundary

Organizations receive agreed programme-level participation/progress signals, not private coaching content, unless the participant explicitly agrees or a clearly disclosed safety/legal exception applies.

Measurement

Pre-defined development goals, participant self-reflection, behavior/application evidence and stakeholder follow-up can be used. Coaching is not represented as the sole cause of business outcomes without an appropriate evaluation design.

Commercial formats

Standalone coaching packs, cohort coaching, executive coaching, manager journey add-ons and post-Prism360 development coaching can be scoped for enterprise engagements.

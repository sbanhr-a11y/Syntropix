VERSIONED INSTRUMENT DOCUMENTATION

Technical manuals,
not marketing summaries.

Thirteen current individual instruments are documented below. Each manual states its source configuration, current construct map, intended use, evidence status, interpretation boundary and change-control rule.

Global interpretation guardrail

WHAT THESE MANUALS DO

Make the product boundary inspectable.

They document what exists today, what a score is intended to support and what still needs empirical evaluation.

WHAT THEY DO NOT DO

Replace empirical validation.

Publishing a technical manual does not create reliability, validity, fairness or predictive evidence. Stronger claims remain withheld until version-specific studies support them.

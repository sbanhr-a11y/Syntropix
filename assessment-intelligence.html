The experience

More than a result screen.

A score is useful only when a person can understand what it represents, recognize it in real situations and decide what to practise next. The Syntropix experience connects structured responses with dimension-level interpretation, capability topology, reflection, AI-assisted development prompts and a practical development protocol.

No population percentile is presented unless a separately validated normative sample supports one. Developmental assessments are not clinical diagnostics and should not be treated as a substitute for accountable human judgement.

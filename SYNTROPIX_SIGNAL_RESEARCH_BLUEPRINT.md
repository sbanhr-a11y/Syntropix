# Syntropix Signal — Research & Validation Blueprint

## Product boundary
Signal is an enterprise employee-experience and engagement listening architecture. It is not a clinical diagnostic, certification scheme, or a claim that survey responses alone establish causal business drivers.

## Market review used for design
- Great Place To Work Trust Index: five workplace-experience areas (credibility, respect, fairness, pride, belonging), 60 statements + two open questions, five-point response scale, benchmarking/certification model.
- Jombay WOW: 16 objective + eNPS + three subjective questions; framework includes empowerment, efficacy, wellbeing, pride and connection, with manager action planning.
- Gallup Q12: manager/team needs framework with extensive criterion research; proprietary content must not be copied.

Signal does not copy proprietary items, scoring, trademarks or certification logic from these providers. Market frameworks are used only to understand category expectations and gaps.

## Proposed construct architecture
1. Engagement outcomes: energy/involvement, discretionary effort, advocacy/eNPS separately, intention to stay.
2. Work design & enablement: role clarity, resources, autonomy, workload sustainability, process friction, learning, career opportunity, recognition.
3. Manager effectiveness: clarity, coaching, feedback, fairness, recognition, psychological safety creation, accountability, development support.
4. Leadership & trust: strategic clarity, communication integrity, confidence, change communication, values-decision consistency.
5. Belonging & fairness: respect, inclusion, voice, interpersonal safety, procedural fairness, equitable opportunity.
6. Team & connection: collaboration, reliability, cross-functional friction, information flow, mutual support.
7. Purpose & alignment: meaning, impact, values alignment, priority clarity.
8. Wellbeing conditions: demands, recovery opportunity, support, work-life interface; explicitly non-clinical.
9. Open voice: strengths, friction, manager practices, employee-proposed improvements.

## Instrument development gates
1. Construct definitions + systematic literature map.
2. Original item generation; legal/IP check; expert content-validity review.
3. Cognitive interviews across roles, levels, geographies and language groups.
4. Pilot; missingness, distribution, ceiling/floor, response-time and item-total diagnostics.
5. Reliability: omega primary; alpha secondary/comparability.
6. Factor structure: EFA in development sample; CFA in independent/held-out sample when design supports it.
7. Measurement invariance before strong subgroup comparisons.
8. Convergent/discriminant and criterion evidence with external measures where licensing permits.
9. Longitudinal sensitivity to change.
10. Norms only after sufficiently large, relevant and representative datasets. Client-relative comparisons must be labeled separately from external norms.

## Analysis architecture
- Favorable/neutral/unfavorable distributions and means with sample sizes.
- Confidence intervals/effect sizes where appropriate; practical significance separated from p-values.
- Association/driver models use transparent multivariable analysis, uncertainty intervals and multicollinearity diagnostics. Do not label associations causal without longitudinal/experimental support.
- Priority matrix combines experience level, association/importance, prevalence and actionability.
- Open text: theme taxonomy, prevalence, secondary sentiment signal, representative redacted verbatims, human review of AI summaries.
- Trend: stable anchor items across waves; instrument-version control.

## Privacy and slicing
- Default reporting threshold: >=5 respondents, configurable upward.
- Threshold is a reporting safeguard, not an anonymity guarantee.
- Apply complementary suppression when intersecting slices could reconstruct a hidden cell.
- Restrict raw-response access; separate identity/administration data from analytics where architecture permits.
- Govern comments for accidental self/third-party identification.
- Slice dimensions may include function, location, level, tenure, work mode and manager group only when business-relevant and legally appropriate.

## Reporting outputs
- Organizational executive report.
- Dimension/driver report.
- Manager/team report when confidentiality threshold is satisfied.
- Segment/slice report.
- Open-text themes report.
- Action portfolio: Protect & Amplify / Fix Now / Build Deliberately / Investigate / Monitor.
- 90-day action pack with owner, action, due date, success measure and review cadence.
- Governed aggregate PDF and CSV/XLSX exports; identifiable raw export is not a default manager feature.

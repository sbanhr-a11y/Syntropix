Experience architecture

From perspectives to a development plan.

01 / INPUT

Relevant perspectives.

Self and appropriate workplace raters can contribute structured feedback around the defined development framework.

02 / SAFEGUARDS

Responsible reporting.

Reporting rules can suppress small rater groups where aggregation would not provide an appropriate confidentiality safeguard.

03 / PATTERNS

Interpret, don't overclaim.

Feedback is used to identify development themes; it is not positioned as a clinical diagnostic or automatic employment decision.

04 / ACTION

30–60–90 focus.

Translate selected priorities into an actionable development plan and review cadence.

Confidentiality note. A minimum group threshold is a reporting safeguard, not an absolute guarantee of anonymity. Appropriate design also considers role context, subgroup size and what respondents disclose.

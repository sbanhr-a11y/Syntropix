How do you respond when work changes?

Everyone learns, handles setbacks, feels pressure, and receives feedback differently. Cognimorph invites you to reflect on how you usually respond in these situations.

The 20 statements take about 10 minutes. Choose the response closest to your usual experience. There are no right or wrong answers.

This developmental self-report is not a diagnosis, measure of intelligence, employment recommendation, or validated comparison with other people. About your results

08 / 08

What this report can and cannot tell you

The scores come from five self-report statements per area and summarize how you chose to answer. This instrument is under scientific development. Its reliability, population norms, fairness and accuracy for individual decisions have not been established.

The examples and action steps are educational illustrations. They should be checked against your work context, what you have actually experienced and the observations of people who know your work. Do not use this report for hiring, medical conclusions or fixed labels about a person.

How the response indexes are calculated

Cognimorph currently uses 20 statements, grouped into four areas with five statements each. You choose one of six agreement options. Eight statements are scored in the opposite direction so that the different responses can be summarized consistently. The results are transformed to a 0–100 response index, where the number describes the answers you selected; it is not a percentage of ability.

Why the numbers need careful interpretation

Two scores that look different are not necessarily evidence of different abilities. The assessment has not yet established reference norms, individual-level precision or validated score thresholds. A meaningful interpretation would also consider what happened in real situations, how others experienced those situations and whether work circumstances helped or hindered your response.

What happens after this report?

Choose one situation that matters to you. Notice what happened, try a realistic change, and ask for a specific observation from someone you trust. You can return to these reflections over time without treating a target number as proof of personal growth. This POC does not use your answers for hiring decisions, does not connect to a live AI service, and does not submit your responses to the production backend.

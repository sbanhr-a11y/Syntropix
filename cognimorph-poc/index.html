How do you respond when work changes?

Everyone learns, handles setbacks, feels pressure, and receives feedback differently. Cognimorph invites you to reflect on how you usually respond in these situations.

The 20 statements take about 10 minutes. Choose the response closest to your usual experience. There are no right or wrong answers.

This developmental self-report is not a diagnosis, measure of intelligence, employment recommendation, or validated comparison with other people. What this report can and cannot tell you

The scores come from five self-report statements per area and summarize how you chose to answer. This instrument is under scientific development. Its reliability, population norms, fairness and accuracy for individual decisions have not been established.

The examples and action steps are educational illustrations. They should be checked against your work context, what you have actually experienced and the observations of people who know your work. Do not use this report for hiring, medical conclusions or fixed labels about a person.

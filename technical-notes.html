ASSESSMENT TECHNICAL NOTES / PUBLIC REGISTER

Make the instrument boundary visible.

A polished report is not a technical manual. These public summaries state what each Syntropix assessment is intended to explore, what evidence status currently applies and what a user or organization should not infer from the score.

Explore assessments →Validation roadmap

CURRENT GLOBAL GUARDRAIL

Developmental indicators, unless stronger version-specific evidence says otherwise.

The current public assessment suite is positioned for developmental reflection and structured conversation. Syntropix does not represent these scores as clinical diagnoses, population percentiles, neurological measurements or guaranteed predictors of performance.

INTENDED INTERPRETATION

Patterns to investigate.

Use scores to focus reflection, development planning and conversation—not to define a person.

CONSEQUENTIAL DECISIONS

Do not use a standalone score.

High-stakes employment use requires job-related evidence, appropriate validation, fairness review, human judgement and applicable-law analysis.

VERSION DISCIPLINE

Evidence attaches to the version tested.

Material item, scoring or population changes can require fresh evaluation before an earlier claim transfers.

PUBLIC INSTRUMENT REGISTER

Ten focused developmental assessments.

Open each note for its current construct focus, intended use and evidence boundary. Statistical coefficients will be published only when version-specific studies support them.

Managerial Effectiveness Index

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Prioritisation, execution, coaching, accountability and the everyday managerial conditions created for others.

Intended use

Manager self-development, coaching and structured cohort development conversations.

Current boundary

Not a standalone promotion, performance-rating or selection decision tool.

Executive Leadership Index

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Broader organizational responsibility, judgement, influence, people leadership and decision quality.

Intended use

Executive reflection, leadership development and coaching.

Current boundary

Comprehensive length does not by itself establish predictive validity or executive potential.

CogniMorph Index

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Adaptability, learning from setbacks and responses when plans, assumptions or operating conditions change.

Intended use

Developmental reflection around behavioural adaptability.

Current boundary

Not a cognitive-ability test or neurological assessment.

Coachability Quotient

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Feedback receptivity, defensiveness regulation, translation of advice into action and proactive feedback seeking.

Intended use

Coaching readiness conversations and development planning.

Current boundary

Does not determine whether a person can or cannot benefit from coaching.

Metacognitive Executive

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Monitoring attention, challenging one's own reasoning and adapting thinking during decisions under pressure.

Intended use

Leadership reflection and decision-development conversations.

Current boundary

Not a clinical measure of cognition, attention or executive functioning.

Cognitive Learning Profiler

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Learning approach, capability-building habits and adaptation of learning strategy as demands change.

Intended use

Learning reflection and development planning.

Current boundary

Not an intelligence, aptitude or learning-disability diagnostic.

Self-Limiting Belief Auditor

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Internal rules and beliefs that may narrow professional choices, confidence or behavioural options.

Intended use

Structured self-reflection and coaching prompts.

Current boundary

Not a mental-health assessment and does not diagnose maladaptive beliefs.

Strategic Inversion Diagnostics

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Assumption testing, downstream consequences and reasoning from the opposite direction before committing to strategy.

Intended use

Strategic-thinking reflection and decision practice.

Current boundary

Not a validated measure of strategic intelligence or business performance.

Commercial Instinct Index

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Commercial judgement, rejection resilience, social initiation, negotiation composure, pipeline discipline and solution creativity.

Intended use

Sales-development reflection and coaching.

Current boundary

Not a standalone hiring or sales-performance predictor.

Communication Signature

DEVELOPMENTAL / EVIDENCE-BUILDING
Construct focus

Clarity, precision, constructive directness, checking understanding, composure and adaptation.

Intended use

Workplace communication reflection and behavioural development.

Current boundary

Describes self-reported patterns; it is not an objective rating of communication effectiveness.

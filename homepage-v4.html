EVIDENCE, WITHOUT THE THEATRE

Strong claims require strong evidence.

Construct evidence ≠ instrument validation

Established research can support what a construct means. It does not automatically validate a proprietary Syntropix instrument.

Human judgement stays in the system

Developmental tools are not clinical diagnostics. Consequential employment decisions require job-related evidence, appropriate human judgment, fairness review and applicable law.

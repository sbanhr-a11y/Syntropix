## 1. Scope and roles
This DPA applies to processing of Personal Data by Syntropix on behalf of Customer under the Agreement to the extent Syntropix acts as processor/service provider. Any processing for which Syntropix independently determines purposes and means must be identified separately and governed accordingly.

## 2. Documented instructions
Syntropix will process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions in the Agreement/SOW/DPA, including transfers, unless applicable law requires otherwise; where legally permitted, Syntropix will inform Customer of that requirement.

## 4. Confidentiality
Syntropix will ensure persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations and access is limited to the service purpose.

## 5. Security
Syntropix will maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the scoped processing. The binding security schedule must list controls actually implemented for the deployment. Provider certifications must not be represented as Syntropix certifications.

## 6. Subprocessors
Customer authorises the subprocessors listed in Schedule B subject to the agreed notice/objection mechanism. Syntropix will impose appropriate data-protection obligations on subprocessors for the services they perform and remains responsible as required by applicable law and contract.

## 7. Data-subject requests
Taking into account the nature of processing, Syntropix will provide reasonable assistance to Customer for requests concerning access, correction, deletion/erasure, restriction, portability, objection or other applicable rights, to the extent Customer cannot fulfil the request without Syntropix assistance.

## 8. Security incidents/personal-data breaches
Syntropix will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, consistent with applicable law and the agreed incident procedure. Do not insert a fixed hour commitment until operational/legal review confirms it. Notices will include available information reasonably necessary for Customer's obligations and will be supplemented as information develops.

## 9. DPIAs and regulator consultation
Syntropix will provide reasonable information and assistance, taking into account the nature of processing and information available, for Customer's required data-protection impact assessments and prior consultation.

## 10. Return and deletion
At termination/end of processing, Syntropix will delete or return Customer Personal Data according to the Agreement, Customer instructions and Schedule C, unless applicable law requires retention. Backup/technical deletion timing must reflect the verified production architecture.

## 11. Audit and information
Syntropix will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with applicable processor obligations and support agreed audits/assessments subject to confidentiality, security, scope, frequency and cost safeguards. Evidence already available should be used before intrusive testing.

## 12. International transfers
The parties will identify transfer locations and applicable transfer mechanism(s) before deployment. Where GDPR Chapter V or another transfer regime applies, the appropriate approved contractual mechanism and supplementary measures, if required, must be completed. Do not assume a transfer mechanism without the deployment facts.

## 13. Local law
The parties will allocate obligations under applicable privacy/data-protection law based on deployment facts, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework where applicable and GDPR/UK GDPR where applicable. This draft does not assert that every deployment is subject to all listed regimes.

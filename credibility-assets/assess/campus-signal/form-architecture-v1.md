# Syntropix Campus Signal — Form Architecture v1

Date: 2026-09-23
Status: PRE-ITEM BUILD HYPOTHESIS

## Target experience

35–40 minutes.

The assessment should feel like a sequence of practical work challenges, not an academic entrance exam.

## Section A — Applied Evidence Reasoning

Target:
- 8 decision units
- ~8–10 minutes

Content:
- 2 data/table items;
- 2 short written-evidence items;
- 2 competing-evidence items;
- 2 insufficient-information / inference-boundary items.

Complexity:
- CX1: 1
- CX2: 4
- CX3: 3
- CX4: 0 in v1 core reasoning unless job family strongly justifies it.

No advanced mathematics.

## Section B — Learn → Apply mini-system

Target:
- 1 fictional system
- 7 scored decisions
- ~8–9 minutes

Candidate receives:
- short rules;
- worked example;
- exception rules.

Progression:
1. direct rule application;
2. combine two rules;
3. exception;
4. transfer to unfamiliar case;
5. new rule/change;
6. resolve conflict;
7. challenge-back.

Primary construct:
Learning Transfer.

Secondary evidence:
Applied Reasoning.

The fictional system must not resemble a known professional certification or require prior domain knowledge.

## Section C — Workplace Judgment

Target:
- 8 decision units
- ~9 minutes

Contexts:
- priorities;
- unclear request;
- mistake;
- help-seeking;
- collaboration;
- deadline;
- customer/colleague;
- escalation.

Communication and ownership evidence can be embedded.

## Section D — Communication / Ownership probes

Target:
- 4 decision units
- ~4–5 minutes

Use only if pilot evidence shows incremental information beyond Workplace Judgment.

Possible examples:
- choose audience/channel;
- write/select concise escalation;
- surface a missed commitment;
- recover after an error.

If redundant, fold into Section C rather than preserve constructs for symmetry.

## Section E — AI Work Judgment

Target:
- 5 decision units
- ~5–6 minutes

Content:
- confidential input;
- unsupported AI claim;
- source verification;
- appropriate task delegation;
- accountable final output.

## Section F — Challenge-back

Target:
- 2 personalised follow-ups
- ~3 minutes

Based on earlier Workplace/AI decisions.

Purpose:
- evidence ownership;
- adaptation after new information;
- integrity support.

## Total target

Initial design:
**34 decision units**

Approximate time:
**37–40 minutes**

If user testing shows burden is too high, the first reduction should be Section D overlap items, not Learn→Apply.

## Complexity distribution

Target form distribution remains approximately:
- CX1 15%
- CX2 40%
- CX3 35%
- CX4 10%

CX4 must arise from conflicting evidence/rules, not hidden professional experience.

## Job-family lens

Campus Signal remains broadly general at launch.

Family lenses may modify a minority of scenarios for:
- technology/data;
- commercial;
- finance;
- operations;
- HR/people;
- customer/service;
- general management.

Do not create 18 separate campus forms at launch.

## Reporting

Primary:
- Applied Reasoning
- Learning Transfer
- Workplace Judgment
- AI Work Judgment

Communication and Ownership should initially appear as evidence themes unless factor/criterion evidence supports standalone scores.

This is a deliberate simplification to prevent over-reporting weak subscales.

## Selection boundary

No automatic Hire/Reject.
No global “employability percentile.”
No claim that one graduate profile is universally superior.

Use:
- evidence for progression;
- areas to verify;
- interview probes;
- evidence confidence.

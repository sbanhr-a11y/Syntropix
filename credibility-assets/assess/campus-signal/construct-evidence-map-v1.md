# Syntropix Campus Signal — Construct Evidence Map v1

Date: 2026-09-23
Status: RESEARCH SYNTHESIS / PRE-ITEM BUILD

## Product question

**What job-relevant evidence does an adult early-career candidate demonstrate of reasoning, learning, workplace judgment and responsible AI-era work readiness before extensive job experience can tell the story?**

## 1. Applied Reasoning

### Why retain
Early-career roles still require interpreting information, drawing supported conclusions and solving unfamiliar problems. Cognitive ability has documented relationships with performance adaptation, especially in dynamic tasks.

### Why constrain it
Recent graduate-selection research raises substantial concerns about:
- adverse impact;
- heavy early-stage screening;
- overestimated historical validity;
- social inequality in access to higher occupations.

### Syntropix design response
Assess **applied job-relevant reasoning**, not abstract “intelligence”:
- provided data;
- short evidence passages;
- inference;
- basic quantitative interpretation;
- identifying insufficient evidence.

Keep it one evidence stream, not the dominant gate.

**Disposition: RETAIN with fairness/weighting safeguards.**

## 2. Learning Transfer

### Why retain
Campus hiring lacks work-history evidence. A direct learn→apply task can observe whether the candidate can acquire a novel rule/system and use it under changing conditions.

Performance-adaptation research finds ability relationships are stronger when adaptation is measured objectively rather than by self-report.

### Syntropix design response
Teach a fictional system inside the assessment, then test:
- rule acquisition;
- combination;
- exceptions;
- transfer;
- adaptation after a rule change.

This avoids asking candidates to self-label as “fast learners.”

**Disposition: RETAIN as a major differentiator.**

## 3. Workplace Judgment

### Why retain
SJTs can provide job-relevant evidence about interpersonal/problem-solving judgment and are generally perceived more favourably when clearly related to work.

### Content
- prioritisation;
- collaboration;
- asking for help;
- escalation;
- mistakes;
- unclear instructions;
- customer/colleague situations.

### Boundary
Do not test hidden corporate etiquette or “culture fit.”

**Disposition: RETAIN.**

## 4. Communication Judgment

### Why retain as a content domain
Early-career failure often comes from choosing the wrong audience, channel, timing or level of detail—not ornamental language.

### Measurement hypothesis
Observe:
- who needs to know;
- what needs to be said;
- timing;
- fact vs assumption;
- concise escalation.

### Fairness boundary
Do not score accent, native-speaker style, eloquence, charisma or expensive-school communication conventions.

### Overlap risk
May collapse empirically into Workplace Judgment.

**Disposition: RETAIN as design domain; factor status unresolved.**

## 5. Ownership & Reliability Judgment

### Why retain as a content domain
Entry-level employers need evidence around commitments, mistakes, handoffs, risk visibility and follow-through.

### Measurement hypothesis
Observe:
- early warning;
- responsibility for error;
- realistic commitment;
- dependency communication;
- recovery action.

### Boundary
Not obedience, long-hours willingness or self-report conscientiousness.

### Overlap risk
May overlap with Workplace Judgment and Execution.

**Disposition: RETAIN as design domain; factor status unresolved.**

## 6. AI Work Judgment

### Why retain
AI is reshaping entry-level work and early-career pathways. Current research increasingly treats AI literacy/readiness as more than tool familiarity; critical evaluation, responsible use and human judgment remain important.

### Measurement hypothesis
Observe:
- when AI is appropriate;
- verification;
- confidentiality;
- source quality;
- disclosure;
- human ownership.

### Boundary
Not:
- prompt tricks;
- model-brand knowledge;
- AI enthusiasm.

**Disposition: RETAIN, with rapid content-review cycle.**

## Method mix

Campus Signal should not depend on one method.

Proposed evidence mix:
- applied-reasoning decisionlets;
- Learn→Apply mini-simulation;
- early-career SJT;
- AI-work SJT;
- challenge-back.

## Construct-overlap hypothesis

The six domains are content specifications, not validated factors.

Particularly test:
- Workplace Judgment ↔ Communication Judgment
- Workplace Judgment ↔ Ownership/Reliability
- Applied Reasoning ↔ Learning Transfer
- AI Work Judgment ↔ Applied Reasoning

Possible final structure may contain fewer broader factors.

## Candidate fairness

Campus must explicitly avoid:
- university prestige as score;
- GPA as hidden proxy;
- business jargon unrelated to entry requirements;
- prior internship experience as unstated knowledge;
- accent/voice scoring;
- socioeconomic familiarity;
- unnecessary speed.

## Research boundary

External literature informs design. It does not validate Campus Signal.

## Conclusion

The six design domains are sufficiently defensible to proceed to method/content architecture. Applied Reasoning must remain proportionate and fairness-monitored; Learning Transfer should receive substantial research attention as a distinctive observable capability.

Date: 2026-09-23

## Core panel — target 12 reviewers

### Early-career hiring / management — 6
1. Campus recruiter / graduate programme TA lead
2. Early-career hiring manager — technology/data/product
3. Early-career hiring manager — commercial/customer
4. Early-career hiring manager — finance/operations
5. Early-career hiring manager — HR/shared services
6. Graduate programme / L&D manager responsible for entry-level development

### Assessment science — 2
7. Psychometrician / I-O or occupational psychologist
8. Graduate-selection / SJT / aptitude-assessment specialist

### Fairness / accessibility — 1
9. Inclusive assessment or digital accessibility specialist

### AI / future of work — 1
10. Responsible AI / workplace AI practitioner

### Education-to-work transition — 1
11. University career-services / employability professional

### Candidate perspective — 1
12. Recent graduate / 0–2 year professional who has completed multiple hiring assessments

## Geography

Initial launch evidence should include substantial India representation.

Where feasible include:
- at least two reviewers with multinational/global hiring experience;
- reviewers exposed to different university tiers and non-elite institutions.

The purpose is to expose hidden assumptions, not to claim cross-cultural validation.

## Special Campus questions

For every item ask:
1. Could a capable candidate answer without prior corporate experience?
2. Does success depend on university prestige, internship exposure or expensive coaching?
3. Is the language more difficult than the reasoning?
4. Does the scenario reflect a genuine entry-level decision?
5. Is there an obviously “professional sounding” correct option?
6. Could a neurodivergent or disabled candidate be disadvantaged by presentation rather than construct?
7. Would a candidate from a non-English-dominant background understand the essential meaning?
8. Does the item test judgment or hidden etiquette?
9. Does it measure something employers actually need at entry?
10. What would you remove to shorten the assessment without losing value?

## Learn→Apply reviewer questions

1. Are the rules understandable without business experience?
2. Is the task application rather than memory?
3. Should the reference remain visible?
4. Are exceptions/precedence clear?
5. Does the new-rule stage genuinely test transfer?
6. Would coaching materially inflate performance?
7. Is the task culturally neutral enough for international adaptation?
8. How should accommodations affect timing?

## Evidence requirement

Every item should receive:
- at least four independent early-career manager/TA ratings;
- method review;
- fairness/accessibility review.

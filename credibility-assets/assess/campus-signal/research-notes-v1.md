## Current evidence themes

### Cognitive testing caution
Recent critical review of graduate/higher-professional selection argues that heavy cognitive screening can exacerbate social inequality and that classic validity claims may be overstated in contemporary graduate contexts.

Syntropix implication:
- keep applied reasoning job-relevant;
- do not make it the dominant early screen;
- monitor subgroup outcomes;
- combine with opportunity-to-perform methods.

### Adaptation / learning
Meta-analytic evidence links cognitive ability with actual performance adaptation, particularly in dynamic tasks, but objective adaptation measures show stronger relationships than self-report.

Syntropix implication:
- prefer direct Learn→Apply performance over a “learning agility” self-report questionnaire.

### Situational judgment
SJT evidence supports moderate predictive validity for construct-relevant outcomes, while heterogeneity means context and criterion match matter.

Syntropix implication:
- keep early-career scenarios construct-congruent and validate against relevant workplace outcomes.

### AI-era entry-level work
Current 2026 work highlights rapidly changing entry-level tasks and the need for AI readiness, critical evaluation and human capability rather than AI use alone.

Syntropix implication:
- measure AI work judgment as applied decisions, not a technology quiz.

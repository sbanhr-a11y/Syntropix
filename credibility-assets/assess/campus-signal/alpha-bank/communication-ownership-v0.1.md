## CO-01 — Status update with uncertainty
Construct: Communication Judgment
CX: CX2

Your manager asks, “Will the analysis be finished by 4 PM?” You have completed about 70%, but one data source is delayed and you do not know when it will arrive.

Which response is strongest?

A. “Yes, I should be able to finish by 4 PM unless something unexpected happens.” **1**
B. “70% is complete. One required source is delayed; if it arrives by 2 PM I expect to finish by 4 PM, otherwise I will update you with the likely impact.” **3**
C. “The analysis is on track except for a delay outside my control.” **1**
D. “I cannot confirm the deadline until the missing data arrives.” **2**

## CO-03 — Commitment at risk
Construct: Ownership & Reliability Judgment
CX: CX2

You committed to complete a task tomorrow. Midway through today you discover it will probably take an extra day.

A. Keep working and raise the issue only if you are certain the deadline will be missed. **1**
B. Alert the affected person now, explain the reason and options, and agree the revised plan before the original deadline. **3**
C. Ask a teammate to help without changing the commitment. **2**
D. Complete a reduced-scope version by tomorrow and explain afterward why it is incomplete. **1**

## CO-04 — Manager gives conflicting instructions
Construct: Communication Judgment
CX: CX2

Your manager tells you to prioritise Task X. Thirty minutes later, a senior leader asks you to drop everything and complete Task Y.

A. Follow the senior leader's request because they have greater authority. **1**
B. Continue Task X until your manager changes the priority. **1**
C. Make the conflicting requests visible quickly, explain the time/impact trade-off, and get the priority resolved rather than guessing. **3**
D. Start Task Y and message your manager that you have changed priorities. **2**

## AR-01 — Weekly support demand

A team handled the following requests:

| Week | Requests | Resolved within target |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 200 | 180 |
| 2 | 220 | 187 |
| 3 | 240 | 192 |

Which conclusion is best supported?

A. Resolution performance improved because more requests were resolved each week. **1**
B. The team became less effective because unresolved requests increased every week. **2**
C. The proportion resolved within target declined across the three weeks. **3**
D. The team needs more employees because demand rose by 20 requests per week. **0**

Why C:
180/200 = 90%, 187/220 = 85%, 192/240 = 80%. D may eventually be true but staffing need is not established.

## AR-02 — Evidence versus explanation

A student-project team introduced a daily 10-minute planning meeting. Delivery errors fell from 12 per month to 7 the next month. During the same month, the team also switched to a simpler checklist.

What is the strongest conclusion?

A. The planning meeting reduced errors because the decline followed its introduction. **1**
B. The checklist caused most of the improvement because it directly controls errors. **1**
C. The combination may have contributed, but the available evidence does not isolate the effect of either change. **3**
D. Neither change can be useful until tested independently. **2**

## AR-03 — Insufficient information

A service team reports that customers receiving a new onboarding email are 15% more likely to complete setup within three days.

Which question is most important before concluding that the new email caused the improvement?

A. Was the email shorter than the old version? **1**
B. Were customers assigned to the new and old emails in a way that made the groups comparable? **3**
C. Did customers say they liked the new email? **1**
D. Was the new email written by a more experienced employee? **0**

## AR-04 — Conflicting indicators

A fictional company tracks two measures after changing a process:

- Average processing time improved from 18 to 13 minutes.
- Rework increased from 4% to 9%.

What is the most defensible interpretation?

A. The change was successful because processing became faster. **1**
B. The change failed because rework more than doubled. **1**
C. The change improved speed but may have reduced quality; both effects need to be considered together. **3**
D. The results cannot be interpreted until customer satisfaction is available. **2**

## AR-05 — Basic capacity decision

A team has 24 hours of available capacity today.

Tasks:
- A: 8 hours, must finish today
- B: 10 hours, deadline tomorrow
- C: 6 hours, must finish today
- D: 8 hours, optional improvement

Assume tasks cannot be partially completed. Which plan protects all today's mandatory work and uses the remaining capacity on the nearest deadline?

A. A + B + C = 24 hours **3**
B. A + C + D = 22 hours **2**
C. B + C + D = 24 hours **0**
D. A + B + D = 26 hours **0**

## AR-06 — Reading a claim carefully

A survey of 120 employees found that people who voluntarily attended an optional training session reported higher confidence afterward than people who did not attend.

Which statement is best supported?

A. The training increased employee confidence. **1**
B. Employees who chose the training reported higher confidence, but the survey alone cannot show whether training caused the difference. **3**
C. Training is most useful for already confident employees. **0**
D. The training should become mandatory so everyone benefits. **1**

# Syntropix Assess — 18 Job-Family Intelligence Maps v1

Date: 2026-09-23
Status: FOUNDATION MAP / PRE-ITEM BUILD

## Purpose

The 18 job families provide contextual lenses for Manager Signal and Campus Signal. They do not create 18 separate psychometric tests.

Each family map informs:
- scenario vocabulary;
- typical outcomes;
- stakeholder patterns;
- decision pressures;
- major risk types;
- AI-use contexts;
- examples for Role Intelligence.

The common product constructs remain stable unless evidence later supports family-specific constructs.

## Occupational intelligence sources

Role Intelligence should map employer JDs against:
- O*NET occupation/task/work-activity/work-context/skills data;
- ESCO occupation/skill relationships;
- Indian NCO/NCS/NOS/Qualification Pack sources where relevant;
- employer-provided JD/context.

Source data informs mapping. Syntropix owns the derived family architecture, role interpretation, scenario design and evidence model.

## 1. Sales & Business Development
Typical outcomes: pipeline, conversion, revenue quality, account expansion.
Stakeholders: prospects, customers, sales leadership, marketing, solution/product teams.
Decision pressures: target pressure, qualification, discounting, forecast confidence, resource focus.
Primary risks: overcommitment, weak qualification, poor escalation, short-term revenue at long-term cost.
AI contexts: prospect research, outreach drafting, account synthesis, proposal support, forecast analysis.

## 2. Customer Success & Account Management
Outcomes: retention, adoption, value realization, renewal/expansion.
Stakeholders: customer sponsors/users, support, product, sales, leadership.
Pressures: customer urgency vs product constraints, renewal risk, escalations.
Risks: overpromising, hidden churn risk, poor expectation management.
AI: customer summaries, support synthesis, adoption analysis, communication drafts.

## 3. Marketing, Brand & Growth
Outcomes: demand, brand health, acquisition, campaign efficiency.
Stakeholders: sales, product, agencies, leadership, customers.
Pressures: speed vs evidence, experimentation vs brand/control risk.
Risks: vanity metrics, weak attribution, inappropriate claims.
AI: content generation, research synthesis, targeting, creative ideation, analytics.

## 4. Product Management
Outcomes: product value, prioritisation, adoption, delivery choices.
Stakeholders: customers, engineering, design, sales, leadership.
Pressures: competing roadmap demands, evidence ambiguity, technical constraints.
Risks: loudest-customer bias, weak discovery, roadmap overload.
AI: research synthesis, prototyping, requirement drafting, analysis.

## 5. Software Engineering
Outcomes: reliable delivery, maintainability, quality, technical risk management.
Stakeholders: product, engineering peers, security, operations, customers indirectly.
Pressures: delivery vs quality, technical debt, incident response.
Risks: insecure shortcuts, fragile architecture, weak escalation.
AI: code assistance, debugging, documentation, test generation; verification essential.

## 6. Data, Analytics & AI
Outcomes: decision-quality data products, models, insight, responsible AI.
Stakeholders: business teams, product, engineering, governance.
Pressures: speed vs data quality, model performance vs interpretability/fairness.
Risks: leakage, unsupported inference, poor validation, privacy.
AI: core work domain; requires high verification/governance context.

## 7. IT, Infrastructure & Cybersecurity
Outcomes: availability, resilience, security, service quality.
Stakeholders: employees, security, vendors, leadership, business functions.
Pressures: uptime vs change risk, incident urgency, access trade-offs.
Risks: security exposure, poor change control, weak incident escalation.
AI: diagnostics, threat analysis, automation; confidentiality and verification critical.

## 8. Finance & Accounting
Outcomes: accuracy, control, insight, compliance, decision support.
Stakeholders: business leaders, auditors, regulators, finance teams.
Pressures: close deadlines, forecast uncertainty, commercial pressure.
Risks: control override, unsupported assumptions, reporting error.
AI: variance analysis, drafting, reconciliation support, forecasting; human verification required.

## 9. HR, People & Talent
Outcomes: talent quality, workforce effectiveness, fair processes, organisational capability.
Stakeholders: employees, managers, leaders, candidates, legal.
Pressures: business urgency vs fair/process-consistent decisions.
Risks: confidentiality, inconsistent treatment, unsupported people inference.
AI: drafting, synthesis, skills mapping, candidate/employee support; high sensitivity around employment decisions.

## 10. Operations & Business Operations
Outcomes: throughput, service, quality, cost, process reliability.
Stakeholders: internal functions, customers, vendors, leadership.
Pressures: speed, capacity constraints, exception handling.
Risks: local optimisation, hidden dependencies, escalation failure.
AI: scheduling, forecasting, process analysis, exception detection.

## 11. Supply Chain, Procurement & Logistics
Outcomes: availability, cost, supplier performance, continuity.
Stakeholders: suppliers, operations, finance, customers.
Pressures: cost vs resilience, lead times, disruptions.
Risks: concentration, contract/control issues, inventory decisions.
AI: demand planning, supplier research, routing, risk monitoring.

## 12. Manufacturing, Quality & EHS
Outcomes: safe production, quality, yield, compliance.
Stakeholders: operators, quality, maintenance, leadership, regulators.
Pressures: output vs safety/quality, downtime, incident response.
Risks: safety compromise, control bypass, repeated defects.
AI: predictive maintenance, quality analytics, documentation; high consequence verification.

## 13. Project & Programme Management
Outcomes: scope/time/cost/value delivery, dependency management.
Stakeholders: sponsors, functional teams, vendors, customers.
Pressures: competing milestones, scope change, resource conflicts.
Risks: hidden dependencies, optimistic reporting, escalation delay.
AI: plan synthesis, risk logs, status reporting, scenario analysis.

## 14. Customer Service & Support
Outcomes: resolution, customer experience, efficiency, escalation quality.
Stakeholders: customers, operations, product, supervisors.
Pressures: queue speed vs resolution quality, difficult interactions.
Risks: premature closure, poor escalation, confidentiality.
AI: response assistance, knowledge retrieval, triage; verification and disclosure rules matter.

## 15. Strategy, Consulting & Corporate Development
Outcomes: insight, strategic choices, transformation, transactions.
Stakeholders: executives, business units, external partners.
Pressures: incomplete evidence, high ambiguity, political/stakeholder complexity.
Risks: false precision, weak assumptions, recommendation without execution realism.
AI: research synthesis, modelling, scenario generation; source quality critical.

## 16. Legal, Risk & Compliance
Outcomes: legal/risk protection, compliant decisions, actionable advice.
Stakeholders: business leaders, regulators, external counsel, audit.
Pressures: speed vs defensibility, commercial objectives vs obligations.
Risks: confidentiality, missed obligations, over-automation.
AI: research/drafting support with strict verification/confidentiality controls.

## 17. Retail, Field & Branch Operations
Outcomes: sales/service, local execution, team performance, controls.
Stakeholders: customers, frontline teams, regional managers, support functions.
Pressures: daily targets, staffing, customer escalation, local exceptions.
Risks: policy inconsistency, cash/control issues, poor people handling.
AI: scheduling, demand insight, customer support, local reporting.

## 18. Administration & Shared Services
Outcomes: accurate service delivery, responsiveness, coordination, process compliance.
Stakeholders: internal users, vendors, managers.
Pressures: volume, SLA, exceptions, competing requests.
Risks: data/privacy errors, poor prioritisation, silent backlog.
AI: drafting, workflow automation, summarisation, knowledge retrieval.

## Family-map metadata standard

Each future role profile stores:
- family;
- occupation source links/IDs;
- level;
- typical outcomes;
- critical tasks;
- stakeholders;
- decision pressures;
- risk exposures;
- AI-use profile;
- candidate product;
- relevant construct emphasis;
- scenario context recommendations;
- mapping confidence;
- human override reason where applicable.

## Design boundary

A family map may change context and scenario sampling. It must not silently change what a score means. Any family-specific scoring/construct change requires separate evidence review.

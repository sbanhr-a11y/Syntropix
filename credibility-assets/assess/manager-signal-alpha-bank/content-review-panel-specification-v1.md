# Manager Signal — Content Review Panel Specification v1

Date: 2026-09-23
Status: EXECUTION SPECIFICATION

## Objective

Obtain independent evidence about realism, construct alignment, scoring quality, fairness/accessibility and AI-era relevance before cognitive testing.

## Core review panel — target 12 people

### A. Practising managers — 7

1. **First-line manager — people-intensive context**
   - 3+ years managing employees
   - examples: customer service, HR, shared services, retail

2. **First-line manager — operational/technical context**
   - examples: operations, technology, manufacturing, supply chain

3. **Mid-level manager — commercial**
   - examples: sales, customer success, marketing

4. **Mid-level manager — corporate**
   - examples: finance, HR, programme management

5. **Mid-level manager — technology/product/data**

6. **Senior manager / functional leader**
   - experience managing managers preferred

7. **Senior manager / business operations leader**
   - significant cross-functional stakeholder responsibility

Aim for more than one industry and more than one organisational environment. Do not recruit only Syntropix-friendly HR contacts.

### B. Assessment / selection specialists — 2

8. **Psychometrician / I-O or occupational psychologist**
   - personnel selection or assessment-development experience
   - SJT experience strongly preferred

9. **Selection-method specialist**
   - structured hiring, assessment centres, SJT/work-sample design or validation experience

### C. Fairness / accessibility — 1

10. **Inclusive assessment / accessibility specialist**
   - experience with disability accommodation, accessible digital assessment or selection fairness

### D. AI / governance — 1

11. **AI governance / responsible AI practitioner**
   - workplace AI governance, human oversight, privacy or model-risk experience

### E. Talent acquisition practitioner — 1

12. **Enterprise TA / hiring-process leader**
   - experience using assessments in real recruiting workflows
   - evaluates usability, hiring-manager value and implementation burden

## Geography

For the first review:
- India should be strongly represented because it is the primary launch market.
- Include at least 2 reviewers with substantial hiring/management experience outside India or in global/multinational environments where feasible.

This does not establish cross-cultural validity. It helps expose assumptions before broader localization work.

## Conflict-of-interest rule

Record:
- relationship with Syntropix/founder;
- paid/unpaid review;
- vendor/client relationship;
- prior exposure to item keys.

Friendly reviewers are allowed but cannot constitute the entire evidence panel.

## Assignment

Manager SMEs:
- all six constructs;
- each reviewer may review a balanced subset to control burden;
- every gold-draft testlet should receive at least 4 independent manager ratings before discussion.

Method specialists:
- review every testlet or a planned balanced sample plus all scoring rules.

Fairness/accessibility:
- review all scenario stems and candidate interaction patterns.

AI specialist:
- mandatory review of AI testlets plus any scenario where AI/integrity rules are relevant.

TA practitioner:
- review full candidate journey, report usefulness and practical deployment.

## Reviewer burden target

Do not ask a manager SME to review 18 full testlets in one sitting.

Recommended:
- 6 testlets per session;
- approximately 45–60 minutes;
- up to two sessions if reviewer agrees.

Method specialists may require longer review.

## Independent phase

Before any group discussion, reviewers submit:
- realism rating;
- expected decision;
- option ranking/effectiveness;
- construct match;
- complexity assessment;
- concern flags.

This prevents consensus pressure from erasing genuine disagreement.

## Resolution workshop

After independent ratings:
1. show disagreement without naming reviewers where practical;
2. discuss assumptions;
3. identify missing context;
4. revise options/rubric;
5. re-rate materially changed items independently.

## Exact manager-SME questions

For every testlet:
1. Have you seen a situation reasonably similar to this?
2. What would make this situation unrealistic?
3. What information is missing that would materially change your decision?
4. What would you most likely do at Stage 1?
5. Rank A–D from strongest to weakest.
6. Explain why your top choice is stronger than your second choice.
7. Which option feels obviously weak or artificial?
8. Does Stage 2 create a real change in the problem?
9. Should the best decision change after Stage 2? Why?
10. Does Stage 3 represent meaningful managerial pressure?
11. What consequence would you expect from the strongest option?
12. What consequence would you expect from the weakest plausible option?
13. Does this scenario test the named construct or something else?
14. Does it assume a particular corporate culture unnecessarily?
15. Would a strong manager in another industry reasonably answer differently?

## Exact TA-practitioner questions

1. At what hiring stage would you use this?
2. Is ~35–40 minutes acceptable for the target population?
3. What would make candidates refuse/drop out?
4. What would a hiring manager actually read in the report?
5. Which evidence would change the next interview?
6. Which output would you distrust or ignore?
7. What client setup would feel burdensome?
8. Would you use this without ATS integration?
9. What compliance/procurement questions would your organisation ask?
10. What would make this product meaningfully different from your current assessment vendor?

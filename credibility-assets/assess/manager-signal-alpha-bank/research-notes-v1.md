# Manager Signal Alpha Bank — Research Notes v1

Status: METHOD NOTES / NOT A VALIDITY CLAIM

## Method anchors

The alpha bank is intentionally scenario-led because:
- OPM describes SJTs as job-related low-fidelity simulations and notes their usefulness for managerial/leadership competencies such as conflict management, problem solving, negotiation and teamwork.
- Response-instruction research shows that “would do” and “should do/effectiveness” instructions can change the constructs captured by an SJT; therefore response instructions are controlled rather than treated as cosmetic.
- Contemporary applicant-reaction research continues to find more favourable reactions to methods perceived as job-related and giving candidates an opportunity to demonstrate relevant ability.
- Recent applied research shows candidate GenAI use in pre-hire assessment is increasing and that clear consequence-based warnings can reduce self-reported usage without materially harming applicant reactions.

## Boundary

These sources justify method choices and research hypotheses. They do not establish that the Syntropix Manager Signal alpha testlets are reliable, valid, fair, predictive or ready for operational hiring decisions.

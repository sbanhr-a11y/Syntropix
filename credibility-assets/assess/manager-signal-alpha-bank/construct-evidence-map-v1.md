# Manager Signal — Construct Evidence Map v1

Date: 2026-09-23
Status: RESEARCH SYNTHESIS / PRE-VALIDATION

## Purpose

This document tests whether the six Manager Signal design domains are defensible enough to proceed into content review. It does **not** claim that Manager Signal has validated six independent factors.

## Method-level foundation

Situational judgment tests are well suited to job-related social/interpersonal/problem-solving content and are widely used for managerial/leadership assessment. OPM explicitly describes SJTs as low-fidelity work simulations and notes their use for conflict management, interpersonal skills, problem solving, negotiation and teamwork.

Recent applicant-reaction evidence continues to favour methods that are visibly job-related and allow candidates to demonstrate relevant capability.

## 1. People Judgment

### Why retain
Managerial work requires repeated decisions about performance, coaching, delegation, conflict and team functioning. These are observable managerial decisions rather than stable personality traits.

### Measurement hypothesis
Scenario evidence can distinguish:
- fact-finding vs premature judgment;
- accountability vs avoidance;
- proportional support/intervention;
- individual vs team consequences;
- appropriate escalation.

### Main contamination risks
- organizational-policy knowledge;
- agreeableness/empathy;
- socially desirable “kind manager” responding.

### Design response
Use competing legitimate goals and avoid obvious morality options.

**Disposition: RETAIN as content domain.**

## 2. Execution & Prioritisation Judgment

### Why retain
Task-related performance remains an important criterion domain in selection research. Managerial value often appears in resource allocation, sequencing, delegation and escalation rather than raw individual productivity.

### Measurement hypothesis
Observe:
- priority trade-offs;
- dependency awareness;
- capacity realism;
- reversibility;
- escalation;
- follow-through.

### Main contamination risks
- conscientiousness;
- industry-specific operating knowledge;
- speed as a false proxy for execution.

### Design response
Supply necessary context and score judgment about trade-offs rather than hustle.

**Disposition: RETAIN.**

## 3. Decision & Evidence Quality

### Why retain
Managerial decisions increasingly require interpreting incomplete/conflicting evidence and updating judgment. AI-enabled work makes source quality, uncertainty and calibration more—not less—important.

### Measurement hypothesis
Observe:
- known vs unknown;
- source/definition quality;
- disconfirming evidence;
- reversible experimentation;
- uncertainty communication;
- evidence updating.

### Main contamination risks
- general cognitive ability;
- numeracy;
- domain knowledge.

### Design response
Keep data interpretation accessible and job-relevant; distinguish reasoning quality from advanced quantitative skill.

**Disposition: RETAIN, with careful cognitive-load control.**

## 4. Stakeholder Judgment

### Why retain
Managers act through interdependent systems. Cross-functional tension, hierarchy, customers and accountability boundaries are core managerial contexts.

### Measurement hypothesis
Observe:
- stakeholder-interest recognition;
- accountability boundaries;
- influence/escalation choice;
- expectation management;
- integrity under authority pressure.

### Main contamination risks
- extraversion;
- political skill;
- culture-specific hierarchy norms.

### Design response
Score decision quality and boundary management, not charisma or deference.

**Disposition: RETAIN, with cross-cultural review.**

## 5. Learning & Adaptive Judgment

### Why retain
Modern work requires adaptation when previous assumptions no longer fit. This domain is deliberately behavioural: it measures what the candidate does with new evidence rather than asking whether they see themselves as adaptable.

### Measurement hypothesis
Observe:
- noticing mismatch;
- revising assumptions;
- experimenting proportionately;
- preserving useful prior learning;
- changing course without ego-defence.

### Main contamination risks
- openness personality;
- novelty seeking;
- general cognitive ability.

### Design response
Use stateful scenarios where new evidence changes the rational decision boundary.

**Disposition: RETAIN; stateful format is particularly important.**

## 6. AI-era Management Judgment

### Why retain
Contemporary research increasingly treats AI literacy as multidimensional—practical, conceptual and ethical—and relevant across management levels. Research also suggests AI's effect on decision quality depends on literacy, trust, collaboration and task context; managers may react differently when AI shifts from advice to autonomous implementation.

### Measurement hypothesis
Observe:
- task suitability;
- human oversight;
- verification;
- privacy/confidentiality;
- model limitation awareness;
- accountable override;
- risk-sensitive automation.

### Main contamination risks
- technology enthusiasm;
- brand/tool familiarity;
- generic compliance knowledge;
- fast-changing AI fashions.

### Design response
Use durable managerial principles and novel scenarios rather than product trivia.

**Disposition: RETAIN as an explicit domain, but expect rapid content refresh and empirical review.**

## Construct overlap hypothesis

The six domains are **content specifications**, not yet psychometric factors.

Potential overlaps to test:
- Execution ↔ Decision Quality
- Stakeholder ↔ People Judgment
- Learning/Adaptability ↔ Decision Quality
- AI Judgment ↔ Decision Quality

Possible outcomes after pilot:
- six empirically distinct dimensions;
- correlated dimensions with higher-order judgment factor;
- fewer broader factors;
- method/testlet effects dominating factor structure.

The technical manual must report what data support rather than force the original blueprint.

## Selection-method complementarity

Manager Signal should not claim to replace:
- structured interviews;
- work samples;
- references;
- job knowledge where required.

It is intended to provide structured pre-interview managerial judgment evidence and to generate targeted verification questions.

## Current conclusion

The six domains are sufficiently distinct and job-relevant to proceed into expert content review and cognitive testing **as design domains**.

They are not yet approved as validated psychometric scales.

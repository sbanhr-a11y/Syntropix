# Manager Signal — SME Review Protocol v1

Study phase: PRE-EMPIRICAL CONTENT REVIEW
Bank: Alpha v0.1

## Reviewer groups

### Managerial SMEs
Target initially: 6–10 experienced managers across multiple industries and management levels.

Desired coverage:
- first-line managers;
- mid-level managers;
- senior managers;
- people-intensive functions;
- operational/commercial functions.

### Method reviewers
Target: 2–3 reviewers with psychometrics/personnel-selection/SJT expertise.

### Fairness/accessibility reviewers
Target: 1–2 reviewers with assessment accessibility, DEI/fairness or inclusive-selection expertise.

One reviewer may cover more than one expertise category only when genuinely qualified.

## Review unit

Each reviewer sees:
- construct definition;
- scenario;
- options;
- proposed CX;
- scoring rubric;
- rationale;
- challenge-back.

Do not show a “correct answer” before independent rating.

## Questions for managerial SMEs

For every stage:
1. Is this situation recognisable in real managerial work?
2. What information would a real manager need that is missing?
3. Which option would you choose?
4. Rank the options from strongest to weakest.
5. Could more than one option reasonably be best? Why?
6. Which option feels unrealistic or obviously poor?
7. What consequence would likely follow each option?
8. Does the scenario become appropriately more complex at later stages?
9. Does the scenario require special industry knowledge?
10. Would the expected answer change materially by country/culture/company policy?

## Questions for method reviewers

1. Is the primary construct clear?
2. Is construct contamination acceptable?
3. Do response instructions match the intended construct?
4. Does the rubric support ordinal partial credit?
5. Are options sufficiently plausible?
6. Does scoring rely on unstated assumptions?
7. Is stateful branching comparable?
8. Is challenge-back measuring adaptation/ownership rather than verbal fluency?
9. Is the intended CX defensible?
10. What empirical analyses should determine item retention?

## Questions for fairness/accessibility review

1. Could irrelevant language complexity affect performance?
2. Does the scenario privilege familiarity with a specific corporate culture?
3. Are hierarchy/deference assumptions necessary?
4. Could disability/accommodation affect performance unrelated to construct?
5. Does timing create avoidable construct-irrelevant variance?
6. Are any demographic stereotypes embedded?
7. Could a reasonable cultural alternative be unfairly scored down?
8. Is the scenario interpretable internationally or does it require localization?

## Independent scoring exercise

Before seeing Syntropix keys, reviewers assign each option:
0 / 1 / 2 / 3

Then compare:
- exact agreement;
- adjacent agreement;
- disagreement pattern;
- rationale.

Do not declare a key “expert validated” merely because average ratings are similar. Resolve substantive disagreement.

## Scenario disposition

For each testlet:
- RETAIN FOR COGNITIVE TEST
- REVISE
- SPLIT INTO MULTIPLE ITEMS
- FAMILY-SPECIFIC ONLY
- RETIRE

# Syntropix Controlled Product Walkthrough Standard — v1.0

Purpose: let enterprise buyers inspect real product behaviour without confusing illustrative content with deployed-client evidence or exposing production data.

## Walkthrough modes
**Mode A — Guided sandbox:** preferred for enterprise demos. Synthetic personas/data only; resettable workspace; no production credentials.
**Mode B — Recorded walkthrough:** version/date stamped; synthetic data; useful for asynchronous due diligence.
**Mode C — Controlled pilot:** real authorised participants under signed scope, privacy terms and measurement plan. This is deployment evidence, not automatically an effectiveness study.

## Mandatory demo banner
“ILLUSTRATIVE / SYNTHETIC DATA — This walkthrough demonstrates product workflow and reporting structure. Values, people, organizations and outcomes shown are not customer results, norms or benchmarks.”

## 30-minute enterprise walkthrough
1. **2 min — boundary:** intended use, evidence status, privacy/confidentiality boundary.
2. **4 min — assignment/admin:** create cohort/assignment, role visibility, invitations, deadlines and status.
3. **5 min — participant experience:** consent/notice, assessment or survey flow, save/restart/timing behaviour where applicable.
4. **6 min — reporting:** show score/pattern interpretation, uncertainty/boundaries, development actions and downloadable output.
5. **4 min — governance:** access, suppression/confidentiality, retention/deletion discussion, audit/admin controls actually implemented.
6. **4 min — product-specific workflow:** Manager Development / Prism360 / Culture Compass / Horizon AC/DC.
7. **3 min — evidence room:** technical note, validation status, provider register and DPA workflow.
8. **2 min — next step:** define pilot question and evidence plan; do not promise an outcome.

## Product-specific proof points
### Manager Development
Show baseline signal → learning/practice → manager/coach follow-through → 30/60/90 review. Do not claim behavioural improvement without measured follow-up.

### Prism360
Show rater groups, self/others pattern, confidentiality/suppression configuration, theme selection and action plan. Explain that thresholds reduce disclosure risk but do not guarantee anonymity.

### Culture Compass
Show survey architecture, governed slicing, suppression, priority matrix, open-text governance and action owner/review cadence. Sample values remain synthetic.

### Horizon AC/DC
Show exercise architecture, assessor observation, behavioural indicators, scoring/calibration, evidence integration and report. Do not imply selection validity until the exact use/exercises/scoring have appropriate job-related evidence.

## Demo-data rules
- Never use a real customer name/logo without written permission.
- Never clone real employee data into demo.
- Synthetic names and organizations must be obviously fictional.
- No “industry benchmark” unless a documented reference sample supports it.
- No fabricated testimonial or ROI.
- Screenshots/video inherit the same evidence labels as live demos.

## Walkthrough QA checklist
Confirm: current release; synthetic dataset; no secrets; no real PII; all links work; labels visible; report boundary visible; mobile/desktop as relevant; failure states rehearsed; WhatsApp/third-party optional paths described accurately; demo reset tested.

## Evidence capture from a controlled pilot
Before pilot launch define: business question, population, baseline, intervention/exposure, follow-up timing, measures, missing-data handling, comparison logic, privacy threshold, qualitative evidence, decision rule and publication permission. Archive the plan before seeing outcomes.

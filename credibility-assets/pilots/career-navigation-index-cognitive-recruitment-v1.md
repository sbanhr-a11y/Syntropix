## Draft participant invitation

Syntropix is testing the wording and usability of a short developmental career questionnaire before a larger research pilot.

We are looking for adults aged 18+ who can read English and are willing to take part in a structured feedback interview about how they understand questionnaire items.

We are interested in a range of career situations, including people who are:
- established in a current role;
- exploring a change;
- actively job searching;
- returning after a career break;
- reskilling or studying toward a career transition;
- following self-employed or non-linear career paths.

This is **not** a job assessment, hiring process, therapy or mental-health evaluation. We are evaluating the questionnaire—not judging the participant.

The session will focus on:
- what each question means to you;
- how you chose your response;
- anything confusing, repetitive, uncomfortable or unrealistic;
- whether the wording makes assumptions that do not fit your career context.

Research participation is voluntary and subject to the study-specific consent/privacy information provided before participation.

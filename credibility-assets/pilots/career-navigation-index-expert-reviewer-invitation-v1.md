## Purpose
Invite qualified reviewers to evaluate the content of the Career Navigation Index version 1.0.0 before participant recruitment.

## Draft invitation

Syntropix is conducting a structured pre-pilot content review of the Career Navigation Index, a 12-item developmental self-report instrument exploring four career-navigation dimensions: concern, control, curiosity and confidence.

We are inviting a small panel of reviewers with relevant expertise in vocational/career psychology, psychometrics, organisational/HR development, career practice, accessibility or plain-language assessment design.

The review is not an endorsement request. We are asking reviewers to identify weaknesses, ambiguity, construct contamination, cultural/context assumptions, reverse-item problems and any wording that could overstate individual agency or drift into broader self-worth constructs.

Expected activity:
- review the construct definitions and 12 items;
- complete a structured item/construct worksheet;
- flag items for retain / revise / retire / cognitive-test;
- optionally join a short follow-up discussion to resolve disagreements.

Reviewer name/affiliation will not be used publicly without explicit permission. Participation in the review does not imply endorsement of Syntropix or validation of the instrument.

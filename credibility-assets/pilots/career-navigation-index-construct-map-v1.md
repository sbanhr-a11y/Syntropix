# Career Navigation Index — Construct & Evidence Map v1

Instrument version: 1.0.0
Status: PRE-EMPIRICAL CONTENT MAP

## Boundary

This map links Syntropix’s intended dimensions to external research traditions. It does not claim that the Syntropix items reproduce, equal or inherit the psychometric properties of another scale.

## Concern

Syntropix intent:
orientation toward future career requirements, planning and maintaining more than one plausible next chapter.

External construct family:
career concern / future preparation within career adaptability.

Primary content risks:
- measuring anxiety/worry instead of preparation;
- privileging highly linear career paths;
- confounding planning with socioeconomic opportunity.

Syntropix item IDs:
1, 2R, 9.

## Control

Syntropix intent:
ownership of career choices and actions that increase future options while acknowledging imperfect conditions.

External construct family:
career control / self-directedness in career adaptability.

Primary content risks:
- implying people control structural labour-market constraints;
- collapsing into general locus of control;
- moralising career outcomes as personal responsibility.

Syntropix item IDs:
3, 4R, 10.

## Curiosity

Syntropix intent:
active investigation of roles, industries and perspectives beyond the current path.

External construct family:
career curiosity / exploration.

Primary content risks:
- conflating opportunity exposure with curiosity;
- favouring people with larger networks;
- measuring novelty seeking rather than career exploration.

Syntropix item IDs:
5, 6R, 11.

## Confidence

Syntropix intent:
belief that a career transition can be broken into learnable/manageable actions and that new skills can be acquired.

External construct family:
career confidence / perceived capacity to handle career-development tasks.

Primary content risks:
- drifting into global self-esteem;
- overstating actual capability from self-belief;
- mixing resilience after setback with competence.

Syntropix item IDs:
7, 8R, 12.

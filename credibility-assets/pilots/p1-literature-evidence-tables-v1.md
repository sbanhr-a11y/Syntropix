# Syntropix P1 Literature Evidence Tables v1.0

Status: **DRAFT COMPLETE / METHOD REVIEW REQUIRED**  
Date: 2026-09-25  
Scope: eight P1 developmental instruments  
Use boundary: U1/U2 developmental and structured-reflection use only.

## Evidence rule

The sources below justify construct relevance, hypotheses and reviewer questions. They do **not** validate Syntropix items, scoring, report bands, norms, predictive utility, fairness, or consequential employment use.

Every source is classified as:
- **Anchor** — strong conceptual or meta-analytic relevance;
- **Adjacent** — informative but not isomorphic to the Syntropix construct;
- **Caution** — evidence that constrains or challenges an interpretation.

No source framework's psychometric properties transfer automatically to a Syntropix instrument.

## 5. Managerial Effectiveness Index™

The MEI shares the ten conceptual domains above but uses five selected items per domain.

**Literature basis:** same external construct families as ELI.  
**Additional evidentiary burden:** because MEI is a short form, content coverage and score precision must be independently demonstrated.

### Short-form hypotheses
1. MEI dimension scores should retain coherent within-domain structure.
2. MEI/ELI corresponding scores should correlate strongly in a planned linking study, but correlation alone will not establish score interchangeability.
3. MEI should preserve enough construct breadth to justify reporting ten dimensions; if not, fewer/broader scores may be scientifically preferable.
4. Any MEI efficiency claim must distinguish completion convenience from psychometric equivalence.

# Cross-instrument construct-collision register

| Collision | Instruments | Risk | Required evidence response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learning/adaptation | CMI ALOR, CLP ADUN/DGAD | Scores may represent one broad favorable adaptability factor. | Discriminant models and external-variable pattern testing. |
| Feedback/flexibility | CMI FRUP, MEA MREG | Construct redundancy. | Item-content review + latent/discriminant evidence. |
| Pressure regulation | CMI CTAP, ELI/MEI CDQL, CII NEGC | Context variants may collapse into general emotional stability. | Compare against broad emotional stability plus context-specific criteria. |
| Agency/proactivity | SLBA EXAG, ELI INSP/ORUI, CII SOIN | Proactive personality may account for much shared variance. | Include proactive-personality comparator in discovery subset. |
| Open-minded updating | CLP ADUN, MEA MREG, SID ASAU | Strong conceptual overlap. | Multitrait modelling; consider rationalising constructs if empirical separation fails. |
| Systems/complexity | SID CXNV/FPRD/SOFC, CLP TRSY | Broad “strategic cognition” factor may dominate. | Behavioural scenario battery and competing-factor models. |
| Social desirability | CII IUIN, ELI EXAC/PDCO/TPSC, SLBA EXAG | Obvious desirable responses can inflate scores. | Reviewer flags, cognitive interviews, impression-management checks where appropriate. |

# Claim boundary

Allowed internal wording:
“The construct architecture is informed by peer-reviewed research traditions and has entered formal content and response-process evaluation.”

Not yet allowed:
- validated
- scientifically proven
- predicts performance
- bias-free
- normed
- measures intelligence/executive function
- identifies high potential
- suitable for selection/promotion

# Acceptance gate

Move literature_map from DRAFT COMPLETE to ACCEPTED only after:
1. source metadata/DOIs are independently checked;
2. construct-domain reviewer confirms conceptual fit;
3. claims/redlines are reviewed;
4. contradictions and null evidence are retained;
5. the final evidence table is version-frozen.

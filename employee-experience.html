MEASUREMENT ARCHITECTURE

Measure the experience. Diagnose the conditions.

Signal uses a proprietary Syntropix framework informed by established organizational research, while keeping a clear distinction between evidence supporting a construct and validation of the Syntropix instrument itself. The instrument should be psychometrically evaluated on client and validation samples before any claim of proprietary predictive validity or normative benchmarking is made.

01 / ENGAGEMENT OUTCOMES

Energy, involvement & intent

Work engagement, discretionary effort, advocacy/eNPS as a separate indicator, and intention to stay. These are treated as outcomes—not automatically as causes.

02 / WORK DESIGN & ENABLEMENT

Can people do good work?

Role clarity, resources, autonomy, workload sustainability, process friction, learning, career opportunity and recognition.

03 / MANAGER EFFECTIVENESS

The local leadership climate

Clarity, coaching, feedback quality, fairness, recognition, psychological safety creation, accountability and development support.

04 / LEADERSHIP & TRUST

Credibility at scale

Strategic clarity, communication integrity, confidence in leadership, change communication and perceived consistency between stated values and decisions.

05 / BELONGING & FAIRNESS

Whose experience differs?

Respect, inclusion, voice, interpersonal safety, procedural fairness and equitable access to opportunity—analyzed with privacy thresholds.

06 / TEAM & CONNECTION

How work happens together

Collaboration, team reliability, cross-functional friction, information flow and mutual support.

07 / PURPOSE & ALIGNMENT

Why the work matters

Line of sight from role to customer/business impact, meaning, values alignment and confidence in priorities.

08 / WELLBEING CONDITIONS

Sustainable performance

Manageable demands, recovery opportunity, support and work-life interface. Signal is not a clinical mental-health diagnostic.

09 / OPEN VOICE

What the scale misses

Open-text prompts capture strengths, friction, manager practices and employee-proposed improvements. Automated text analysis must remain reviewable by humans.

SURVEY DESIGN

A measurement system, not a popularity poll.

Core census

Recommended starting architecture: approximately 45–55 carefully tested Likert items, 1 eNPS item kept analytically separate, 3–4 open-text prompts, and only business-relevant demographics. Final item count follows pilot reliability/factor analysis—not arbitrary length.

Pulse mode

Short rotating modules can track priority drivers between census waves. Stable anchor items preserve trend comparability; rotating items answer current business questions.

Response scale

Five-point agreement or frequency scales with explicit labels, plus “not applicable” where conceptually valid. Favor neutrally worded, single-idea items and avoid double-barrelled or leading statements.

ANALYSIS STANDARD

From scores to defensible decisions.

Psychometric quality

Pretest through cognitive interviews; pilot; inspect missingness, ceiling/floor effects and item distributions; estimate internal consistency using omega (and alpha for comparability); examine item-total relationships; use EFA during development and CFA on an independent/held-out sample when sample size and design support it. Test measurement invariance before treating subgroup score differences as directly comparable.

Driver analysis—with restraint

Use correlations and multivariable models to identify associations with engagement/intent outcomes, with uncertainty intervals and multicollinearity checks. Longitudinal or experimental evidence is required before labeling a driver causal. Importance can combine effect size, prevalence of unfavorable experience and actionability.

Trend & significance

Show change with sample sizes, confidence intervals/effect sizes where appropriate, and response-rate context. Flag practical significance separately from statistical significance. Avoid ranking tiny score differences as meaningful.

Open-text intelligence

Theme coding, sentiment as a secondary signal, representative verbatims with privacy review, theme prevalence and linkage to quantitative dimensions. AI-generated summaries must be labeled and human-reviewed before executive publication.

REPORTING ARCHITECTURE

Every report ends with action.

Executive report

Participation, overall experience profile, outcome indicators, strengths, urgent priorities, material subgroup gaps, driver evidence, text themes, risk notes and 90-day action portfolio.

Manager report

Manager Effectiveness profile, team experience patterns, strengths to preserve, behaviors to change, recommended coaching/development actions and follow-up pulse measures—only when confidentiality rules are satisfied.

Action taxonomy

Protect what works. Fix now high-impact friction. Build structural capability over 6–12 months. Investigate ambiguous signals. Every recommendation records owner, due date, measure and review cadence.

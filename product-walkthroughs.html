Prism360

Self/other differences are development signals, not truth scores. Confidentiality safeguards depend on group size, role context and comment handling.

01

Configure the rater architecture

Define self, manager, peer, direct-report and other groups around the development purpose.

02

Suppress unsafe granularity

A numerical threshold reduces disclosure risk but does not guarantee anonymity when role context makes identity obvious.

03

Choose the few development themes that matter

Translate recurring patterns into behaviour experiments and stakeholder follow-up rather than a judgement label.

Reporting boundary. Minimum group thresholds are safeguards, not guarantees of anonymity. Potentially identifying free-text comments require separate handling.

The Culture Compass

Pulse data becomes useful when governance links signal, action owner, due date and follow-up measurement. Illustrative values are not market norms.

01

Pulse with a defined decision

Know what leaders could do differently based on the result before asking employees to spend attention on a survey.

02

Use a governed action taxonomy

Protect what works, fix immediate friction, build structural capability and investigate ambiguous signals.

03

Close the loop

Assign owner, due date and follow-up pulse rather than ending with a presentation deck.

Measurement boundary. Survey movement is interpreted with organizational context. A before/after difference is not automatically represented as proof that Syntropix caused a business outcome.

Horizon AC/DC

Assessment-centre evidence should separate observation from inference. The controlled walkthrough emphasizes simulation evidence, assessor calibration and developmental output.

01

Map role-relevant competencies to exercises

Simulation design should be tied to the actual role and intended use rather than a generic leadership model.

02

Capture observable behavioural evidence

Assessors record evidence before drawing conclusions, then calibrate across exercises and assessors.

03

Retain accountable human judgement

No hidden automated score replaces appropriate job-related evidence, validation, fairness review and accountable human decision-making.

Consequential-use boundary. Selection, promotion or succession use requires the evidence and governance appropriate to that specific role, population and decision context.

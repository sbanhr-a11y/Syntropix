Our point of view

Human capability is not one score.

Evidence without theatre

Useful claims should earn their confidence.

Syntropix separates research-informed construct design from instrument-specific validation. Developmental tools are not clinical diagnostics, and consequential decisions require job-related evidence, human judgement, fairness review and applicable law.

Research-informed

Established literature helps define what an instrument is designed to explore.

Validation earned separately

Reliability, validity and fairness evidence must be built for each instrument before stronger claims are made.

Human judgement retained

Technology supports interpretation and development; it does not replace accountable human decision-making.

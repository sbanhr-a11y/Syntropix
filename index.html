EVIDENCE, WITHOUT THE THEATRE

Strong claims require strong evidence.

Research-informed. Validation earned separately.

We use established research to define what an assessment is designed to explore. Each Syntropix instrument must then build its own evidence through testing, reliability analysis and validation before stronger psychometric claims are made.

Human judgement stays in the system

Developmental tools are not clinical diagnostics. Consequential employment decisions require job-related evidence, appropriate human judgment, fairness review and applicable law.

360° Feedback Cycles

How to run a Prism360 survey ▼
  1. Plan. Decide who's being reviewed (the "subject") and pick a deadline — 10 business days is the default and works well for most cycles.
  2. Add raters. Below, add the subject's manager, direct reports, peers, and stakeholders. Direct Reports, Peers, and Stakeholders each need at least 3 people or their category won't be shown separately in the report — it'll be folded into a combined "Other Raters" group instead, to protect anonymity.
  3. Create the cycle. Click "Create Cycle" below. Nothing is emailed yet at this point.
  4. Open it. Click "Open & Send Invites" on the cycle once you're ready — this is the point of no return for that cycle: every rater gets their email with their personal survey link.
  5. Let it run. Reminders go out automatically at the halfway point and 24 hours before the deadline. You don't need to do anything unless you want to nudge everyone manually with "Send Reminder Now."
  6. It closes itself. Once the deadline passes, the system automatically checks if enough people responded, scores everything, generates the report, and emails it — usually within 15–30 minutes of the deadline. You'll also see its status update here.
  7. Testing? Tick "This is a test cycle," use your own email for every rater category (add "+self", "+manager", "+peer1" etc. before the @ in a Gmail address — they all land in your one inbox as separate addresses), then use "Run Now" to close the cycle immediately instead of waiting for the deadline. When you're done, click "Delete Test Data" — nothing from a deleted test cycle is kept anywhere.

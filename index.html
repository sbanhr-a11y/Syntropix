EVIDENCE, RESPONSIBLY

Strong claims should earn strong evidence.

We separate established research from evidence for each proprietary instrument, preserve human judgement and avoid turning developmental tools into clinical or deterministic claims.

RESEARCH-INFORMED ≠ AUTOMATICALLY VALIDATED

Transparency before theatre.

Syntropix uses established research to define constructs and development questions. Each proprietary instrument must then build its own evidence through testing, reliability analysis and validation before stronger psychometric claims are made.

SLATE / EVIDENCE

Human judgement stays in the system.

Consequential employment decisions require job-related evidence, appropriate human review, fairness consideration and applicable law.

PLUM / REFLECTION

Development is contextual.

A score is not an identity. Interpretation should consider role, environment, experience and the behaviour a person actually wants to change.

SECURE & RELIABLE / GLOBAL CAPABILITY

Human data deserves engineering discipline.

Syntropix is designed around explicit access boundaries, purpose-limited data handling and governed releases. We do not borrow a hosting provider’s certification and present it as our own.

Review security & privacy principles →Enterprise Trust Centre →

Infrastructure providers maintain their own controls and certifications within their documented service boundaries. Syntropix remains responsible for application configuration, authorization, data handling and operating discipline.

01

Least-privilege access

Privileged application secrets remain server-side, with explicit role and workflow boundaries for sensitive operations.

02

Purpose-bound people data

Assessment, feedback and pulse-check data are handled according to the use case, visibility rules and retention purpose—not exposed simply because the platform can expose them.

03

Privacy-aware reporting

Confidential feedback and subgroup reporting use suppression and context safeguards to reduce disclosure risk.

04

Governed releases

Production changes pass regression and runtime safety gates so presentation work cannot silently alter assessment logic.

05

Global deployment readiness

Enterprise deployments can be configured around applicable privacy, data-processing and cross-border requirements for the client context.

Scientific Annexure & Dimensional Architecture

Theoretical foundation and cognitive behavioral criteria of the CogniMorph Index™.

1. Proprietary Mindset Definition

Mindset is an individual’s internal cognitive operating architecture—a dynamic, neuro-adaptable framework of implicit assumptions that governs how the brain filters reality, metabolizes error and friction, translates autonomic stress into execution stamina, and decodes evaluative signals from the environment into strategic action.

2. Dimensional Subscales

  • Cognitive Plasticity & Epistemic Flexibility (CPEF): Belief in dynamic synaptic capacity vs static talent limits; willingness to restructure mental models.
  • Error Salience & Limbic Friction Tolerance (ESLFT): Conscious error appraisal (Error Positivity / Pe waveform) and dopamine-effort pairing during unmastered tasks.
  • Psychophysiological Stress & Allostatic Valence (PSAV): Cognitive appraisal of autonomic arousal as metabolic energy mobilization rather than toxic distress.
  • Decentered Evaluation & Synergistic Growth (DESG): Decoupling personal identity from critical feedback and leveraging peer mastery as an operational blueprint.

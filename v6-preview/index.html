EVIDENCE, RESPONSIBLY

Strong claims should earn strong evidence.

We separate established research from evidence for each proprietary instrument, preserve human judgement and avoid turning developmental tools into clinical or deterministic claims.

RESEARCH-INFORMED ≠ AUTOMATICALLY VALIDATED

Transparency before theatre.

Syntropix uses established research to define constructs and development questions. Each proprietary instrument must then build its own evidence through testing, reliability analysis and validation before stronger psychometric claims are made.

SLATE / EVIDENCE

Human judgement stays in the system.

Consequential employment decisions require job-related evidence, appropriate human review, fairness consideration and applicable law.

PLUM / REFLECTION

Development is contextual.

A score is not an identity. Interpretation should consider role, environment, experience and the behaviour a person actually wants to change.

Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Who we are

Syntropix ("we," "us," "our") provides manager assessment and development tools, including psychometric diagnostics, 360° feedback, and AI-assisted coaching, at syntropix.in. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

2. What we collect

  • Identity data: name, email address, and mobile number you provide when creating an account or starting an assessment.
  • Assessment data: your responses to psychometric and manager-effectiveness questions, and the scores/archetypes generated from them.
  • AI coaching interactions: messages you send to the AI coach and the profile context used to generate responses. These are sent to our AI provider (currently Groq, and optionally Google Gemini) solely to generate your reply.
  • Payment confirmation: we receive confirmation of payment from PayPal, but Syntropix does not store your card or PayPal account details directly — that is handled entirely by PayPal.
  • Technical data: basic session information (a login token) used to keep you signed in.

3. Why we collect it

  • To generate your assessment report and score.
  • To email you a copy of your diagnostic report as a PDF.
  • To power the AI coaching conversation you initiate.
  • To verify payment before granting access to a paid assessment.
  • To maintain your login session securely.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your assessment responses for advertising.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is currently stored in a private, access-controlled Google Sheets database operated by Syntropix, and is being migrated to a dedicated database (Supabase/PostgreSQL) as part of an ongoing infrastructure upgrade. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are cryptographically hashed with a unique salt per user.

5. Third parties we share data with

  • PayPal — to process payment. See PayPal's own privacy policy for how they handle your payment details.
  • Groq / Google Gemini — to generate AI coaching responses. Only your prompt and assessment context are sent, not your name, email, or payment information.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail/Sheets) — used as our current email delivery and data storage infrastructure.

We do not sell or rent your data to any third party for marketing purposes.

6. Your rights

You can request to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for AI coaching processing at any time (this will simply stop the AI coach from functioning for your account; your report data is unaffected).

To exercise any of these rights, email support@syntropix.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

We retain your assessment data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion at any time as described above.

8. Children's data

Syntropix is intended for working professionals and is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our infrastructure and practices evolve (for example, as we complete our migration to a dedicated database). Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@syntropix.com

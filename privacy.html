Privacy Policy

This policy describes the categories of personal data Syntropix may process when you use our website, assessments and related services, and the purposes for which that information is used.

Information we may process

  • Account and contact information you provide, such as name and email address.
  • Assessment responses, scores and report information generated when you use an assessment.
  • Information submitted through feedback, coaching or organizational programmes where those features are used.
  • Transaction confirmation needed to provide access to paid services. Payment credentials are handled by the applicable payment provider rather than being intentionally collected by Syntropix.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

Why we process information

We use information to provide requested services, generate assessment outputs, maintain accounts and access, communicate about a service, process or verify transactions, improve reliability, and protect the platform from misuse.

Service providers

Syntropix uses technology and service providers to operate parts of the platform. Information is shared with providers only where needed for the relevant service or operational purpose. The providers and architecture may evolve as the platform develops; we do not treat a provider's certifications as certifications of Syntropix itself.

Assessment information

Assessment data can be sensitive in a workplace context. Syntropix assessments are positioned as developmental tools unless a specific instrument and use case has separately established evidence appropriate to another purpose. They are not medical or clinical diagnostics.

Retention and security

We aim to retain personal information only for legitimate service, legal and operational purposes and apply access and security controls appropriate to the platform. No online system can promise absolute security.

Your requests

Subject to applicable law and the context in which data is processed, you may contact us about access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with you.

Children

The services described here are intended for adults and professional or organizational use, not for children.

Changes

We may update this policy as services, infrastructure and legal requirements evolve. The version published on this page is the current website policy.

Contact

Privacy and data questions can be sent to team@syntropix.in.

Discounts

Where shown, eligible research-consent savings are 15% and eligible third-or-later distinct-assessment savings are 20%. Discounts do not stack; the single higher eligible discount applies. What is not included

A Professional assessment purchase does not include coaching, medical or clinical diagnosis, therapy, employment certification, a subscription, or unlimited assessment attempts unless the relevant product page expressly states otherwise.

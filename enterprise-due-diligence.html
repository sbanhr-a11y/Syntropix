Privacy and legal readiness

India. Syntropix tracks the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. The Rules have phased commencement dates; contracting and operations should follow provisions applicable at the deployment date rather than implying that every future-dated obligation is already in force.
EU/EEA. Where Syntropix acts as processor for an enterprise controller, the DPA template is structured around GDPR Article 28. Where an international transfer mechanism is required, the applicable European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful mechanism must be identified for the actual data flow.

Enterprise review checklist

  • Define the exact product/workflow, participant population and client decision being supported.
  • Classify personal data, free text, assessment/feedback responses and report outputs involved.
  • Define controller/processor or equivalent roles for the actual flow.
  • Agree participant, client-admin, assessor, coach and Syntropix administrative visibility.
  • Agree retention, deletion/return, incident contacts and rights-request cooperation.
  • Review subprocessors and cross-border transfer mechanism where applicable.
  • Confirm assessment interpretation boundaries and any proposed consequential employment use.
  • Define baseline, measures and follow-up before making outcome claims.
